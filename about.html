Who am I ?

A developer who also studies the planet.

I'm Maruf Al Misbah, a full-stack web developer and UI/UX designer from Jashore, Bangladesh. I design in Figma and build with React, Next.js, Laravel and PHP, so a project stays consistent from the first wireframe to the final deployed page.

Since 2023 I have been studying Environmental Sciences at the University of Chittagong. I care about climate, clean air and clean water, and I like building software that can help people understand them.

In 2026 I finished certificates in web design and front-end development, and I now take on freelance projects for clients worldwide: business websites, web apps and portals.

How I work

From idea to launch.

Four clear steps, so you always know what comes next.

01

Discover

We talk about your goal, your users and your deadline. I ask the questions that save time later.

02

Design

I sketch the layout and design the screens in Figma, so you see the site before any code is written.

03

Build

I turn the design into clean, responsive code with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, PHP or Laravel.

04

Launch

I test on real devices, fix the rough edges, publish the site and stay around for support.

Problem solver

I find the bug, then fix it for good.

Debugging is a method, not luck. Here is how I work, and a small game to test your own bug radar.

  1. 1

    Reproduce

    Make the bug happen on purpose, on the same device and browser.

  2. 2

    Isolate

    Cut the problem down to the smallest piece that still breaks.

  3. 3

    Fix

    Change the cause, not just the symptom.

  4. 4

    Test

    Check the fix and everything around it, on phone and desktop.

  5. 5

    Prevent

    Add a check or a note so it cannot quietly come back.

Problems I fix

Slow pages

I measure first: image size, scripts, fonts. Then I cut what is not needed.

Broken on mobile

I test real screen widths and fix overflow, tap sizes and layout jumps.

Forms that do not send

I trace the form from click to inbox and add clear error messages.

Not found on Google

I check titles, headings, meta tags and the sitemap.

Layout and style bugs

I find the rule that wins and fix it at the source, not with hacks.

Security holes

I check inputs, links and dependencies for the common mistakes.
